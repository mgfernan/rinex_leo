# IGS Workshop 2024

In this workshop two activities were taken:

- Presentation of the first cut at LEO-PNT extension for RINEX files
- Attendance to the RINEX Splinter session on Friday July 5th 2024

## Main takeaways from the Splinter session

These main points were discussed during the splinter session

- A sensible approach to define the interface would be to wait for commercial
missions to publish the ICD and know exactly the characteristics of the signal.
- Satellite ID naming seems to be the first point to discuss before further
development in the RINEX adaptation. In this direction, even the satellite
constellation identified would have to be extended as well: more than 26
constellations of satellites are likely to appear in the foreseeable future. In
this context, a two-digit for the constellation and a 5 digit for the ID could
cover all constellations (incl. mega-constellations).
- Another point of discussion is to know the translation between Satellite ID
and Signal ID (i.e. PRN)
